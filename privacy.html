Privacy Policy

At SOUND SHOP (referred to as "we," "us," or "our"), we provide premium audio infrastructure. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This policy outlines how we handle data in accordance with federal law and global security standards.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when purchasing audio licenses or registering for our services. This includes: Name, Email Address, and Billing Address. We do NOT store full credit card numbers on our servers.

2. Payment Processing Security

All payments are processed through secure, PCI-DSS compliant merchant service providers (including PaymentCloud and Authorize.net). Your financial data is encrypted using 256-bit SSL (Secure Sockets Layer) technology. SOUND SHOP maintains a secure environment by utilizing tokenization, ensuring your raw payment data never touches our local infrastructure.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your information is used strictly to:

  • Process and deliver digital audio assets.
  • Verify licensing agreements for professional use.
  • Provide technical support and order updates.

4. Data Protection Rights

In accordance with the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to request access to the data we hold, request deletion of your information, or opt-out of future communications at any time.

5. Third-Party Disclosure

SOUND SHOP does not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties. This does not include trusted third parties who assist us in operating our website and conducting our business, so long as those parties agree to keep this information confidential.
